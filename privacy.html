OneView — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

OneView is a personal app that lets a content creator see the statistics of their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts in one place. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data we access

Only after you explicitly connect an account and grant permission, OneView reads the following data through the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram (Meta) APIs:

  • Profile information: display name, username and profile picture.
  • Audience data: follower or subscriber count.
  • Content statistics: views, likes and comments on your videos and posts, and their publication dates.
  • Comments on your own videos and posts (with OneView Pro): author name, text and date.

OneView never acts on your accounts on its own. The only changes it makes are the comment replies and comment deletions that you explicitly request in the app. It never reads or sends private messages.

2. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored in the secure storage of your device (iOS Keychain). Statistics and their history are stored locally on your device. OneView has no user account, and your social media data is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

3. Subscriptions

OneView Pro is purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google; OneView never sees them. To confirm that a subscription is active, the app sends the store's transaction identifier or purchase token to OneView's verification server, which checks it with Apple or Google and returns only whether the subscription is active and when it expires. This identifier is not linked to your name, email or social media accounts, is used only to check your subscription, and is not stored beyond what is needed for that check.

4. How data is used

Data is used solely to display your statistics, charts and Home Screen widgets inside the app. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose, and it is never sold or shared.

5. Third-party services

When you connect an account, you sign in directly with Google (YouTube), TikTok or Meta (Instagram). Their own privacy policies apply to that sign-in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

OneView's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. OneView also uses the YouTube API Services.

6. Data retention and deletion

Statistics history is kept on your device for up to 35 days. You can delete all data related to an account at any time by long-pressing it in the app and choosing Disconnect: its tokens and statistics are immediately removed from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining data.

You can also revoke OneView's access at any time from your account settings:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Meta: Settings → Apps and websites (Facebook and Instagram)

7. Children

OneView is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always available on this page, with its date at the top.

9. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository: github.com/lixnew2/OneView/issues.
