OneView — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

OneView is a personal app that lets a content creator see the statistics of their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts in one place. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data we access

Only after you explicitly connect an account and grant permission, OneView reads the following data through the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram (Meta) APIs:

  • Profile information: display name, username and profile picture.
  • Audience data: follower or subscriber count.
  • Content statistics: titles, thumbnails, views, likes and comments of your videos and posts, and their publication dates.
  • Comments on your own videos and posts (with OneView Pro): author name, text and date.

OneView never acts on your accounts on its own. The only changes it makes are the comment replies and comment deletions that you explicitly request in the app. It never reads or sends private messages.

2. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored in the secure storage of your device (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Statistics, their history, your goals and your settings are stored locally on your device. OneView has no user account and no server of its own: your social media data is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

3. Subscriptions

OneView Pro is purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google; OneView never sees them. The app checks whether your subscription is active directly with Apple or Google, on your device. No purchase information is sent to the developer.

4. How data is used

Data is used solely to display your statistics, charts, goals, insights (best time to post, top and flop content), reports and Home Screen widgets inside the app, and to show you notifications. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose, and it is never sold or shared.

4b. Notifications, reports and demo mode

  • Notifications: milestone and "post taking off" alerts are computed on your device from your own statistics and shown as local notifications. No push service or server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Reports: the shareable report image is generated on your device. It is only sent somewhere when you choose an app or a person in your device's share sheet.
  • Demo mode: the demo shows fictional sample data generated inside the app. It does not access any account.

5. Third-party services

When you connect an account, you sign in directly with Google (YouTube), TikTok or Meta (Instagram). Their own privacy policies apply to that sign-in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

OneView's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. OneView also uses the YouTube API Services.

5b. Google user data (YouTube)

When you connect a YouTube account, OneView requests three Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel name, profile picture and subscriber count, and the list of your own videos with their titles, thumbnails, views, likes and comment counts.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read your channel's daily views, likes, comments and subscribers gained or lost, shown as charts and period totals.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to read the comments on your own videos and, only when you tap the corresponding button, post a reply or delete / reject a comment.

Data access: OneView only accesses the raw data listed above, for the channel you choose to connect. It does not access any other Google service or account data.

Data use: Google user data is used only to show you your statistics, charts, goals, best-time-to-post insights, reports, comment inbox and widgets inside the app, and to compute on-device notifications. It is never used for advertising, profiling, lending or to train AI/ML models.

Data transfer: Google user data, raw or aggregated, is not transferred, sold or shared with any third party, and is not sent to the developer. The only exception is a report image that you choose to share yourself through your device's share sheet.

Data protection: all requests go directly from your device to Google over HTTPS. OAuth tokens are stored in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore); statistics are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Data retention and deletion: statistics history is kept on your device for up to 35 days. Disconnecting the YouTube account in the app (long-press → Remove) immediately deletes its tokens and data from your device; uninstalling the app removes everything. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

OneView's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and deletion

Statistics history is kept on your device for up to 35 days. You can delete all data related to an account at any time by long-pressing it in the app and choosing Disconnect: its tokens and statistics are immediately removed from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining data.

You can also revoke OneView's access at any time from your account settings:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Meta: Settings → Apps and websites (Facebook and Instagram)

7. Children

OneView is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always available on this page, with its date at the top.

9. Contact
