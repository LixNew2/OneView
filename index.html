OneView: all your creator stats, in one app.

OneView is an app for content creators, free to start. It brings the views, likes, comments and followers of your YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts together in one dashboard, helps you set goals and find your best time to post, and turns your week into a report you can share in one tap.

YouTube TikTok Instagram Try the demo, no account needed

What OneView does

A simple dashboard for your own accounts, in English, French and Spanish.

∑

One total for everything

See your combined views and followers across every connected account, for the last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time.

↗

Trends you can explore

Interactive charts for each account and each video. Slide your finger along the curve to read the exact value.

⚑

Goals for every account

Set as many goals as you want, like 50K followers on TikTok or 10M views on YouTube, and see when you will reach them at your current pace.

◷

Best time to post

A day-by-hour heatmap of when your posts perform best, plus your top and flop content ranked by views or engagement rate.

⇪

Shareable reports

Turn your week or your month into a story-ready image and share it to Instagram, WhatsApp or a client in one tap.

🔔

Milestone alerts

Get notified when you pass 10K followers or when a post takes off with 3× your usual views. Computed on your phone.

▦

Accounts and workspaces

Connect several accounts per platform and group them into workspaces, for example personal channels and client accounts.

✉

Comments

Read and reply to comments on your YouTube and Instagram content, with a badge when new ones arrive.

▣

Home Screen widgets

Pick a workspace, accounts and period, and keep your numbers on your Home Screen, on iPhone and Android.

Private by design

You sign in directly with Google, TikTok or Meta. Your tokens, statistics, goals and notifications stay on your device: no account to create, nothing sold or shared. Reports are generated on your phone and only leave it when you choose to share them. Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Disconnect an account at any time to delete its data.
