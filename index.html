Private by design

You sign in directly with Google, TikTok or Meta. Your tokens, statistics, goals and notifications stay on your device: no account to create, nothing sold or shared. Reports are generated on your phone and only leave it when you share them. Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Disconnect an account at any time to delete its data.
