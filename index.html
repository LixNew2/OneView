OneView: all your creator stats, in one app.

OneView is an app for content creators, free to start. It brings the views, likes, comments and followers of your YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts together in a single, simple dashboard.

YouTube TikTok Instagram

What OneView does

A simple dashboard for your own accounts. Nothing more, nothing less.

∑

One total for everything

See your combined views and followers across every connected account, for the last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time.

↗

Trends you can explore

Interactive charts for each account and each video. Slide your finger along the curve to read the exact value.

▦

Accounts and workspaces

Connect several accounts per platform and group them into workspaces, for example personal channels and client accounts.

◎

Latest content

Your three latest videos, Shorts, TikToks or Reels with their views, likes and comments.

✉

Comments

Read and reply to comments on your YouTube and Instagram content, with a badge when new ones arrive.

▣

Home Screen widgets

Pick a workspace, accounts and period, and keep your numbers on your iPhone Home Screen.

Private by design

You sign in directly with Google, TikTok or Meta. Your tokens and statistics are stored only on your device: no account to create, nothing sold or shared. Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Disconnect an account at any time to delete its data.
